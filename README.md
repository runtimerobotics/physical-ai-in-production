# Physical AI in Production

A 36-slide industry briefing (October 2026): where robot foundation models are shipping today, where they're still in pilots, how to take a model to production, and what to watch next.

By **Lentin Joseph**, CEO of RUNTIME Robotics and author of 11 books on ROS and robotics.

**View it:** https://runtimerobotics.github.io/physical-ai-in-production/

Navigate with the arrow keys, the buttons at the bottom, or by swiping. Link to a slide with `#n`, for example `#8` for open VLA models or `#15` for the videos.

## Contents

1. What Physical AI is, how a VLA model works, its three maturity levels, and a 2024–2026 timeline
2. The models powering real robots (π0.6, DeepFleet, DYNA, Helix, Skild, GR00T, Gemini Robotics, Dream-1), a list of open VLAs you can download today with a release-timeline chart, and a Dream Labs Robot Playground demo
3. Deployments: production, humanoid pilots, videos, home robots, and the race in China and India
4. Business models, the enabling layer, and why it's harder than chatbots
5. Taking a model to production: the production loop, spec and data, build vs. fine-tune vs. API, the evaluation ladder, layered safety architecture, on-robot deployment, staged rollouts and the data flywheel, a ROS 2 reference architecture, and a readiness checklist
6. Outlook, takeaways, how to get started, sources, and contact
